# Code of conduct

EveryLocale is built by and for people who work across languages and cultures. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews is expected to make that work welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, including with people writing in a second language.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it the same way.
- Respect people's time: search before opening an issue, and keep threads on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, language, accent or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's report content feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and temporarily or permanently block participants who do not follow this code. The decision considers the severity, whether the behavior continued after a warning, and its effect on others.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
